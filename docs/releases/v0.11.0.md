Fixes found by testing the editor end to end in REAPER, and a few behaviour changes.

- **×2** copies the clip's notes into the new half. A new REAPER clip (Loop source on,
  one pass) lengthens and grows like any clip and keeps looping at its new length; it no
  longer turns on Repeat phrase by itself. A clip looping its source shows its repeats.
- **MPE** needs two or more channels with their own expression. A lead on one channel with
  pitch bend keeps the channel you give new notes.
- **Same-pitch notes** follow Ableton: a note moved over the start of another replaces it,
  over its end shortens it.
- **Shift + click / drag on the ruler** plays from there (scrub), as in Ableton.
- **Fit** (opening, X, Z) uses the real piano roll height, rows down to 10 px, and folds when
  the range is too tall. F keeps the zoom. Only Alt + wheel sets the saved row height.
- Ctrl+click no longer leaves a hidden copy; short notes can be moved; right-click acts on
  the clicked note; moving a fader keeps the selection; Shift+Left stops at one grid step.
- Modulation: new lanes stay editable after writing, a flat lane no longer flickers 63/64,
  Ctrl+D and paste keep curves intact, pasted recorded CC joins a curve lane and the old
  value returns after it, Steps painting leaves no stray points, Curve / Steps is saved,
  and a CC74 lane works on MPE clips.
- A repeat duplicated to another track no longer blocks edits of its phrase.
