Notes keep everything that belongs to them when you move, copy, transpose or delete them: polyphonic aftertouch, REAPER notation and, in MPE recordings, each note's pitch bend, pressure and timbre.

- A moved MPE note keeps its slide and no longer starts detuned by the bend its channel was left at.
- Copies and new notes that would share a channel with a sounding MPE note get a free channel.
- Every note keeps the bend, pressure and timbre it started with, even when a neighbour on its channel moves away or is deleted.
- Chord channels and ordinary clips behave as before.

See "What travels with a note" in the user guide.
